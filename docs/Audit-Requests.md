# Audit Requests

If you are a manufacturer of a VISCA-compatible PTZ controller, I would be happy to evaluate your product or products at no charge and include the results within this project.

This test evaluation will follow the documented [Testing Approach](Testing-Approach.md).
Results will be published as a review of the VISCA-compatible features sought as part of this project, specifically including range and resolution of the PTZ control speed parameters, supported call presets, and any other notable functional observations found during testing - in line with the audit results already collected and published.

## Requirements

1. Shipping: Manufacturer is responsible for shipping and return shipping, with any desired insurance included, both to/from my location in the USA.
	1. Please include a pre-paid return shipping label in the original shipment.
2. Duration: Manufacturer must allow up to 30 calendar days (~4 weeks) from receipt of the controller for evaluation before return shipment.
	1. This can be extended for longer, with the agreement of Manufacturer, if desired for further firmware development and re-testing.
3. Email support from Manufacturer must be available during the evaluation period.
	1. Provide the preferred email address to use in your initial request.
4. Manufacturers are encouraged to be prepared to further develop and produce any iterative firmware versions for additional testing when issues are identified, particularly where firmware updates may resolve observed behaviors and results.
5. Repeated tests across multiple firmware versions are allowed and encouraged.
	1. Updated firmware for testing must include a set of release notes that details changes between firmware versions.
	2. Controller must still be in my possession (not yet returned) for repeat testing, or a new controller must be re-provided and shipped for repeat testing (see #1).
6. Testing is performed over VISCA over RS-232 serial connections only.
7. Discontinuation: I reserve the right to discontinue this evaluation service at any time, without notice and for any reason.
	1. In the event of discontinuation, any controller provided for evaluation that remains in my possession and is owned by the manufacturer will be promptly returned to the manufacturer.

## Publication

1. Evaluation results are independent and will reflect the behavior observed during testing, including both successful and unsuccessful tests.
2. Participation in an evaluation does not imply endorsement, certification, or a passing result.
3. Results will identify the specific hardware and firmware versions evaluated and will not necessarily represent the behavior of other hardware or firmware versions.
4. Manufacturers will be given an opportunity to review draft results for factual errors before publication.
	1. Manufacturer review is limited to factual corrections and clarification of technical information.
	2. Manufacturer will not have editorial control over test results, findings, or conclusions.
5. Previously published results may remain available as a historical record when subsequent firmware or hardware revisions are evaluated.
6. Where appropriate, manufacturers may provide comments or additional information that will be linked to or included alongside the published results.
7.	Audit / test results will only be published within this project for firmware versions that are publicly released and available at the time of the evaluation.
	1. With agreement from Manufacturer, interim / under-development results may be additionally and separately listed and notated as such until the associated firmware is publicly released.

## Process

1. Manufacturers are strongly encouraged to start by opening an issue for an audit request within this project: [Audit Request](https://github.com/ziesemer/visca-audit/issues/new?template=audit_request.yml)
	1. Manufacturers without a GitHub account may also email me at [online@mark.ziesemer.com](mailto:online@mark.ziesemer.com), and I will open an issue on their behalf.
2. I will email the listed email contact from `online@mark.ziesemer.com` to privately coordinate shipping details, tracking #'s, and other logistics.
3. Shipping accountability:
	1. I will update the public request status to "received" and "returned", as applicable shipping events occur.
	2. Manufacturer is requested to provide an update (comment) on the public request with any shipped / received activity from their perspective.
4. Manufacturer may provide any updates via comment to the public request during the evaluation.
	1. Manufacturers without a GitHub account may also request that I share updates on their behalf.
5. Manufacturer will be given an opportunity to review draft results for factual errors before publication outside of the public issue request.
6. Audit results committed from the evaluation will be linked to the public request.
